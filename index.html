Schengen Area (Europe)

Covers France, Greece, Italy, Switzerland, Spain, etc.

  • Center: VFS Global, Chhaya Center, Thamel.
  • Docs: NOC from employer, 6-month bank statement (strong balance), €30K Travel Insurance, Flight/Hotel bookings.
  • Time: Apply at least 45 days prior.

Japan

Requires a Temporary Visitor Visa for Tourism.

  • Center: VFS Global Kathmandu or Embassy in Panipokhari.
  • Docs: Detailed daily itinerary (Form provided by embassy), Bank statement, NOC.
  • Time: 5-10 working days.

Bali (Indonesia)

E-Visa (B211A) recommended over unpredictable VOA.

  • Process: Apply online via official portal or local travel agent in KTM.
  • Docs: Passport copy, Digital Photo, Return flight.
Official E-Visa Portal

The Maldives

Visa on Arrival (FREE) for Nepali Passports.

  • Process: Granted upon landing at Malé airport.
  • Mandatory: Fill the IMUGA digital declaration 96 hours before departure.
  • Docs: Confirmed prepaid hotel booking.
IMUGA Declaration

China (Lijiang, Beijing)

Requires "L" Tourist Visa. Must apply at the Chinese Visa Application Service Center (CVASC) located at Rising Mall, Kamaladi, Kathmandu. Requires physical submission of passport, photos, and flight/hotel bookings.
